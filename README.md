# Autonomous Venture Engine (AVE) — v0.2 "Autonomous Venturing"

An autonomous venture team that discovers, validates, builds, launches and operates small digital
businesses **for operators who already have an edge** — an audience, a domain they know, channels
they can use, accounts in their own name. AVE works while you sleep, on your assets, with your money,
inside hard limits you set. It is not passive income, it holds none of your money, and it takes no
share of what your ventures earn.

> **v0.2 names the category: Autonomous Venturing.** AVE runs at a declared level of autonomy
> (1 Assisted, 2 Supervised, 3 Conditional, 4 High; Level 5 is not offered). The level is a real
> setting in the database: choosing one applies its limits, higher levels are refused until the
> owner's own ledger has earned them, and published ledgers state the level they were run at.
> See `docs/FIVE_LEVELS.md` and `docs/POSITIONING.md`. Line: *Hands off. Eyes on. Brakes always.*
>
> **v0.1 was the council reshape** (see `docs/POSITIONING.md`, `docs/BOUNDARIES.md`, `docs/DOGFOOD_RUN.md`).
> Four things changed: the buyer is an operator, not a dreamer; AVE never takes custody or carry;
> every venture asset lives in the owner's own accounts; and the first job of the system is to prove
> its own loop in public on a $100 dogfood venture.

**v0 architecture: Hyperagent agents are the workforce, Supabase is the shared memory and the
rules-based guardrail, a single static page is the Owner Command Center.**

## Setup (about 20 minutes)

1. **Supabase.** Create a project (or pick an empty one). Run `supabase/migrations/0001_ave_core.sql`
   then `0002_reshape_operator_edge.sql` and `0003_autonomy_levels.sql` in the SQL editor (or via the Supabase MCP `apply_migration`). Enable **Email (magic link)** auth.
   Add the dashboard URL to Auth → URL configuration → redirect URLs.
2. **Dashboard.** Host `dashboard/index.html` anywhere static (GitHub Pages, Netlify, Vercel) or open
   it locally. Enter the project URL + anon key, sign in by magic link. First sign-in creates your
   owner row and default policy. **Fill in "Your edge"** (who already listens to you, what you know,
   which channels and accounts you have) — discovery is refused until it is complete. Then click
   **+ Capital** to allocate your first budget. The money stays in your accounts; the ledger only tracks it.
3. **Hyperagent.** Save the eight agent drafts (Orchestrator + seven specialists). Pin the "AVE Config"
   document (project ref, dashboard URL, owner id) to all of them. Enable the Supabase integration on each;
   GitHub on the Builder. Turn on live mode for the Orchestrator (daily is the cost-safe default).
4. **Autonomy level.** New owners start at Level 2 (Supervised). The dashboard's "Autonomy level"
   card shows what agents may do without asking and what you stay at the wheel for; "Fine-tune this
   level" tightens individual limits. Level 3 unlocks after 3 completed ventures and 1 published
   ledger; the database checks. The numbers below are the Level 2 preset.
   In the dashboard, set your limits. Defaults: agents may self-approve ≤ $25 per spend and
   ≤ $100/day; $500 per venture; 2 active ventures; owner must approve launch, kill, scale and any
   external commitment. **Dogfood mode is on by default**: every venture is capped at $100 with stage
   caps of $40 / $20 / $40 and the wedge is digital products. Turn it off once you have published a
   ledger snapshot you are happy with.
5. **Your accounts, not ours.** Connect the owner's own Gumroad / Lemon Squeezy / Paystack / Stripe,
   GitHub, hosting and (later) Meta ad account. Agents register every asset in `venture_assets`; the
   database refuses launch until the storefront and repo are registered as owner-controlled.

## The guardrail contract

**Level rules (0003), enforced in SQL:**
* `policies.autonomy_level` is 1 to 4. Level 5 is refused by a CHECK constraint and a trigger.
* Changing the level applies that level's preset limits and logs a decision. Only the owner can change it.
* Moving up to Level 3 or 4 is refused until `fn_level_evidence()` shows the owner's ledger has earned it. Moving down is always allowed.
* Hand-tuning a limit keeps the level and sets `level_custom`.
* `fn_build_ledger_snapshot()` stamps the level on every published ledger.

**Reshape rules (0002), enforced in SQL:**
* No discovery until `owner_edge` is complete. A venture must fit the owner's edge (`edge_fit_score`).
* `venture_assets.owner_controlled` must be true; launch needs an owner-controlled storefront/payment account and repo/hosting.
* `solution_shape` must be in `policies.allowed_solution_shapes` (digital products first; micro-SaaS opt-in).
* Dogfood mode: $100 venture cap, per-stage caps, every venture flagged `dogfood`.
* Spend purposes that read like holding, moving or investing anyone's money raise an error; "custody", "escrow", "revenue share", "carry", "pooled capital", "crowdfund" are forbidden keywords in every policy.
* `agent_runs` records model, tokens and `cost_cents`; `v_cost_of_intelligence` puts it next to cash and revenue.
* `public_ledger_snapshots`: the Treasurer drafts, only the authenticated owner publishes, anon may read published rows.

* Agents **file** spend requests; the database **decides** (`auto_approved` / `pending_owner` / `rejected`)
  using policy limits, venture budgets, available capital, daily caps, category allow-lists, keyword
  deny-lists, venture state and the kill switch. The status an agent writes is ignored.
* An `expense` can only be recorded against an approved request with headroom. Ledger rows are immutable.
* Revenue counts only when `verified` with an external payment reference. Forecasts never enter the ledger.
* Only an authenticated owner (`auth.uid()`) can approve, reject, move capital, edit policy or flip the
  kill switch. Service-role and MCP sessions have no `auth.uid()` and are refused by the triggers.
* Launch, kill and budget increases require an approved `approvals` row of that type.

Run the tests: `python3 tests/test_guardrails.py`.

## What v0 deliberately does not do

* No payment processing, no custody, no revenue share — ever. Ventures sell through the owner's own storefront or payment links; the Treasurer only reconciles. The platform is paid by flat subscription, outside this ledger (see `docs/BOUNDARIES.md`).
* No "passive income" — in copy, reports or code. AVE is a team for operators with an edge.
* No separate Operations agent — the Builder maintains, the Distributor nurtures, until a venture earns one.
* No multi-tenant SaaS shell — one owner per Supabase project. Multi-owner is a schema-compatible later step
  (RLS is already per-owner).
* No guarantees. The system reports actuals separately from forecasts, always.
