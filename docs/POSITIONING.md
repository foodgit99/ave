# AVE positioning (v0.1, after the council reshape)

## One line

**An autonomous venture team for operators who already have an edge.** It discovers, validates,
builds, sells and runs small digital products on your audience, in your accounts, with your money,
inside limits you set — while you sleep.

## Who it is for

| Buyer | Their edge | Why AVE fits |
|---|---|---|
| Agency owner | Client roster, ad accounts, creative team, a niche they understand | Spin up and test productized offers and digital products for their niche without pulling staff off client work |
| Creator / newsletter / community owner | An audience that already listens | Turn repeat audience questions into paid digital products, tested on the list first |
| Domain expert (accountant, lawyer, coach, consultant) | Knowledge a general model lacks; a professional network | Package know-how as templates, tools and productized services; AVE does the shipping and selling |
| Small founder team | A live product, customers, a brand | Run adjacent-product experiments in the margins |

**Not for:** people with no audience, no domain and no channels who want "AI to make money while I
sleep". The council's arithmetic says that buyer loses: an opportunity a general model can find for
them it can find for everyone, and $500 of ads cannot buy an edge. We do not sell to that buyer, and
we do not use the language that attracts them.

## What we say and never say

| We say | We never say |
|---|---|
| "A venture team that works while you sleep" | "Passive income" |
| "On your audience, in your accounts, with your money" | "Allocate capital and we deploy it" |
| "Hard limits you set; the database enforces them" | "Guaranteed", "returns", "ROI you can expect" |
| "Every dollar and every kill, in a ledger you can publish" | "Revenue share", "we win when you win" (we charge flat) |
| "Most ventures die; the loop is built to kill cheap and learn" | Success rates, income claims, testimonials we didn't verify |

## Wedge and sequence

1. **Digital products** (templates, guides, prompt packs, small tools) sold through the owner's own
   storefront — near-zero ops, instant payment, kill/scale loop in days.
2. **AI services and productized services** delivered with agent help, fixed price, owner's brand.
3. **Automation tools** for the owner's own niche.
4. **Micro-SaaS** — opt-in per owner once a product has paying customers and warrants support/uptime.

## Pricing (platform)

* **Flat subscription**, in the range the closest comparable has anchored ($49–99 / month), billed to
  the operator for the engine and dashboard. Intelligence cost (agent runs) is shown per venture and
  either included up to a cap or passed through at cost — never marked up as "performance".
* **No carry, no percentage of capital, no revenue share.** This is the differentiator and the legal
  line (see `BOUNDARIES.md`). The owner's ventures are the owner's; AVE is a tool they pay for.
* **Founding operators**: 3–5 design partners at a founding price in exchange for a public ledger they
  are willing to have published. Their receipts are the launch marketing.

## Proof before pitch

AVE's own first customer is its builder. The first job of every deployment is a `DOGFOOD_RUN.md`
cycle: a $100 venture, a 48-hour target, a published ledger — kills included. Convergence across five
simulated personas is a hypothesis; a public ledger is evidence.

## Honest marketing rules (for the Distributor and for AVE's own site)

* Lead with what the owner brings. "Your list, your niche — we do the shipping and selling."
* Show the ledger, not the dream. Link to published snapshots, including failures.
* Name the base rate: most indie products earn nothing; AVE is built to find that out for $100, not $5,000.
* Every claim traces to a verified number or it is cut.
